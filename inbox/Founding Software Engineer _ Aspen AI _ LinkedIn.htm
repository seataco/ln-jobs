About the job

Aspen AI was created to solve one of the most challenging business needs: acquisition, conversion, and management of customers. By automating the first half of the customer journey, we empower businesses to focus on nurturing converted customers while eliminating unnecessary interactions. Our technology ensures businesses receive qualified leads, ready to be transformed into loyal customers.


  • An agile, elite team: Everyone is held accountable and expected to make meaningful contributions to the direction of projects and features.
  • High-impact development: We build next-generation applications that engage millions of leads and save companies millions of dollars in staff hours.


At Aspen AI, you will become part of an elite team of software engineers committed to developing cutting-edge technology that leverages AI to revolutionize two critical areas: digital advertising (Meta, Google, Bing) and client communications (phone calls, texts, and emails). Our AI operates autonomously, handling both advertising and client communication without human involvement. Joining us at this early stage gives you the opportunity to make foundational decisions that will shape the future of our product.


Your Responsibilities:

  • Collaborate in the architecture, design, and development of new and existing features to ensure our solutions meet the highest quality.
  • Solve complex problems with creativity and precision to consistently deliver value to our customers
  • Experiment with and adopt cutting-edge technologies, tools, and processes to optimize your own and the team’s efficiency. Demonstrate a genuine interest in continuous learning and improvement.


Requirements:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Minimum GPA of 3.7.
  • 1+ years of industry experience building software.
  • Proficiency with C#, Python, React, SQL, and a web framework—preferably .NET 8 / .NET Core.
  • Strong programming experience.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • A passion for excellence and excitement about future technologies.


Preferred Skills:

  • Experience with TypeScript.


Disclaimer: This role is not for those seeking a traditional 9-to-5 job. It offers a unique and rewarding challenge, where you will take ownership of key features and build them from the ground up. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and want to be part of something transformative, we’d love to hear from you.
